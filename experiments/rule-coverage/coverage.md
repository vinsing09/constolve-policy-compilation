# Policy rule coverage (telecom, 24-scenario suite)

Generated by `coverage.py` from `rules.csv`, the control policy (`experiments/rule-ablation/policies/control.md`) and the first 24 cases of the suite. No model calls.

**51 rules. Covered 20, not covered 20, not checkable 11. Of the 40 checkable rules, 20 (50%) are checked by at least one of the 93 criteria.**

**Covered is not passed.** A covered rule has a criterion that would fail if the agent skipped the call; it says nothing about how often agents pass it.

## Method

- **Rule**: one directive telling the agent what to do, not do, or in which order or condition (`rules.csv`, text checked verbatim against the stated policy lines). Not counted: descriptions of the domain and of what tools return, "X can be used to check" hints (lines 232, 238, 285, 344), "Refer to" cross-references, and the data-limit options paragraph (313-319, whose actions are D04 and P06). A directive restated in a later section with the same condition and action is one rule with both line numbers (16;171, 106;118, 156;301, 253;360).
- **Covered**: at least one criterion checks it. Tie rule (rule-ablation prereg, made strict): the rule directs a call to the criterion's tool under a condition the scenario's starting state meets, and every tool in the criterion's `any_of` is one the rule directs. `tool_called_with` ties to a must-call clause, `tool_not_called` to a must-not-call clause; "before" constraints are not tied separately.
- **Not checkable**: only observable in what the agent says (or reasons). The grader reads tool calls only.
- **Differences from the prereg table**: (1) line 324 is not covered: its only candidate criterion (scenario 2, set_network_mode_preference) starts at `4g_only`, which is neither 2G nor 3G; (2) the two `diagnosed_before_handoff` criteria (any_of check_sim_status, check_status_bar) tie to no rule, since a status-bar check is not a call the policy directs; the prereg's R5 counted them; (3) line 118 is merged with 106 (check the bill is overdue first), which no criterion grades.
